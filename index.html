About Me

Engineer. Researcher.
Problem Solver.

I am an Electrical and Electronic Engineer from Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), currently serving as Executive Engineer (Current Charge) at the Bangladesh Rural Electrification Board (BREB) — one of the country's largest power utilities.

In my role, I lead system protection and design operations for Rajshahi Division: overseeing planning, fault investigation, equipment coordination, and technical standardisation to ensure reliable power delivery to rural communities.

Alongside my professional duties, I am actively pursuing an MSc at RUET and conducting research at the intersection of renewable energy, machine learning, and smart grid technologies. My long-term goal is a PhD at a top-ranked university, where I can advance intelligent energy systems that address real-world power challenges in developing nations.
